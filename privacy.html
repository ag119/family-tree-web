Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

Family Tree ("the app", developer: Nest) lets people build a shared family tree. This page explains what data the app handles.

What we collect

  • Account: your Google account name, email address, and profile photo, used to sign you in.
  • Family data you enter: names, photos, dates of birth/death, professions, and notes about family members you add.
  • Device data: a push-notification token, plus crash and usage diagnostics via Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics.

How we use it

To run the app: sign you in, show and sync your family tree, send invite links, and (with your permission) notify you about birthdays and anniversaries. We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party ads.

Information about other people

When you add a relative you are entering personal data about someone else. Only add information you have the right to share. Any relative can claim their own profile and edit it, and anyone can ask us to remove their data using the contact below.

How data is stored and shared

Data is stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Authentication) and encrypted in transit. Family details are visible to other signed-in members connected to the same family tree. We share data only with Google as our infrastructure provider.

Your choices

You can request deletion of your account and its data at any time — see Delete your account. Deletion removes your sign-in account, your own profile and its photos, and your settings within 30 days; connections and information other members added about shared relatives may be retained.

Children

Family Tree is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly create accounts for them.
